Funding and Cash Runway

This is where the plan attacks its own numbers hardest. Every year-end cash balance across six years is positive, but eight individual months inside those years are not. The lowest month is May 2028, about $778,960 short. It matters because a year that adds up can still contain a month with nothing in the bank, and two of the model's own checks read review by design, both here.

Every year positive, eight months not

The first round is sized around the month-by-month road, not the annual total. A $500,000 borrowing facility would narrow the worst month to $340,891 across five months but would not close it. What closes it is a bigger first round, slower spending, or an earlier 2028 round.

The biggest single dependency

Federal research credits worth $2,351,025 across the plan are the largest single dependency. Removing them moves the worst month to $1,336,747 and doubles the overdrawn months from eight to sixteen. The cure is recording engineers' time from the first hour of work.

A month of runway at the decision

Cash at the end of 2027 divided by 2028's monthly cost is 1.06 months, against a twelve month threshold the plan sets for itself, so this check reads review. The honest reading is that the round funds choice, not survival: the business turns profitable in 2028.

Price off 2028, not 2027

Priced off 2028 the round is $3.5M at $30,000,000 and the founder keeps 52.69 per cent. Priced off 2027 the same money lands at $8,414,163 and the founder keeps 20.73 per cent. The only thing that forces the worse column is running out of cash.
