WHAT THIS BUSINESS IS
The product4orm runs the daily proof a regulated business owes when it holds somebody else's money. Every morning it balances the books, saves each version with who approved it and when, builds the regulator's filing from that record, and produces the inspection bundle on one button.
The duty it sells againstSince 8 September 2025, any payment company registered with the Bank of Canada that holds customer money must know every day whose money it holds and how much, check that against where the money is kept, and show the evidence on request. The duty is the company's own and cannot be passed to the bank.
The six revenue linesThe six things 4orm sells: subscription, keeping the record, rebuilding records nobody kept, the two filing packs, a second regulator, and usage meters. The Revenue Lines tab gives each one a price, a take-up rate and an on and off switch.
What it is notNo money movement of any kind. The system reads balances and evidences them. That boundary keeps 4orm outside the registration regime and outside the penalty ceilings that fall on its customers.
HOW THE MODEL IS BUILT
Customers come from the marketCustomer counts are management assumptions, typed by size band and year on the Cohorts tab. Every figure that follows from them is calculated.
One selector moves the whole planOne selector on the Control Panel. It moves how many customers are won each year and how many of them buy extra lines. Price, customer losses and the cost base are changed where they are typed, not here.
Nine switches, all on the Control PanelThe Control Panel holds nine switches. Each of the six revenue lines has its own switch and price factor, once on the Revenue Lines tab and again on the Map to Seed tab, which runs the months up to the round.
Costs have driversEvery overhead line is a fixed amount plus a rate multiplied by headcount, customers or new hires. Change the hiring plan and insurance, tooling and recruitment all move by themselves.
Revenue is customers times priceA customer live at the end of a year contributes half of that year. Hiring uses the same convention, so a mid year hire costs half a year. There is no growth rate assumption anywhere.
2026 is a part year2026 is five months long, August to December. Pay, overhead and revenue all scale on it.
Two escalators, not onePay rises three per cent a year from 2028. There is one list price per size band, and it rises once, on 1 January 2028. A firm keeps the price it signed at for as long as it stays, so the lower 2027 price is a closing price rather than a discount. Overhead rises separately at two and a half per cent.
Contingency is a real cost lineTwenty per cent of the cost base in 2026, stepping down to eight per cent by 2031.
Two rounds, and the first must stand aloneFirst money in is $2,050,000, paid in five instalments across 2026 and 2027. The next round is $3,500,000 in 2028. The test that matters is whether the first money reaches ten customers who will speak to an investor without it. A $500,000 loan sits behind that, switched off here.
Grants are modelled, never committedEight grant programmes are modelled and none has been awarded. Switch one turns them off, and the plan still reaches the next round, on a larger loan.

THE EIGHT NUMBERS A PLAN LIKE THIS USUALLY FLATTERS, AND WHERE THIS ONE SITS
TaxTax is eleven per cent on the first five hundred thousand dollars of profit and twenty three per cent above that. Both rates are modelled, and early losses are used to cut the tax bill in the first profitable years. Ignoring either moves the answer by a lot.
GrantsEight programmes are modelled and none of them is awarded. Mitacs is not among them, because Mitacs pays the academic intern and the company contributes rather than receives. Switch the grants off on the Control Panel and the plan still reaches the seed.
The exchange rateOne point three seven four, the Bank of Canada daily average. It is the easiest number in a workbook to leave stale, so it is stated on the Control Panel, on this tab and on the sources tab, and it is the only rate used anywhere.
Gross marginGross margin runs from 64 per cent in 2027 to 78 per cent in 2031, held down in the early years by evidence reconstruction and implementation, which carry a fifty five per cent delivery cost.
Sales and marketingSales and marketing is 11 per cent of revenue against a twenty per cent median. The Channel and Sales tab gives the reason: just over half the selling is done by partners paid out of the revenue they bring.
The research creditThe research tax credit is thirty per cent of qualifying engineering pay plus eighty per cent of contract payments, plus the Alberta innovation employment grant. Every rate is sourced, and it is the one line running in the company's favour.
ChurnCustomers lost each year are counted by size band rather than as one average: command three per cent, enterprise five, mid market eight, small seven, partner eight.
The model sets out what has to be true: how much of which market, at what price, against what costs, on how much capital. Each can be tested against what actually happens. The two most likely to fail are the share of the market won and how fast a new customer gets value.
