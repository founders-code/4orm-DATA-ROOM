THE FIVE THINGS THIS MODEL SAYS
The pre-seed carries the business to the seed conversationTwo million and fifty thousand dollars across five tranches, a low point of about six hundred and twenty thousand at the end of 2026, and cash at the end of 2027 covering about four months of what 2028 costs. A five hundred thousand dollar borrowing facility at eight per cent sits behind that, switched off in the base case. Switching it on lifts the low point to about one million one hundred thousand and carries the plan to the fourth quarter 2028 seed with real margin rather than none.
Customers are derived from the market, not assertedEvery customer number in this workbook is an addressable population multiplied by a penetration rate. Four of the eight populations have a published count behind them, one is listed but not counted, and three have no published count at all. All of that is named on the Market tab.
The second regulator is where the platform claim gets testedProperty management trust accounting runs on the same engine, the same three building blocks and the same five screens. If it needs the underlying data structure rebuilt, this is several products wearing one name and the expansion case fails.
The business funds without grants, and it shouldEight programmes are modelled and none is awarded. Switch them off on the Control Panel and the plan still reaches the seed. Mitacs is not among them, because it pays the academic intern rather than the company.
Profitability arrives in 2031, on twenty three peopleEarnings turn positive in the sixth year, on four hundred and eleven customers and a cost base that has been costed at published rates rather than at convenient ones. Every rate that decides it carries a source on the Sources tab.
AND THE FOUR THINGS IT DOES NOT SAY
It does not say the penetration rates are achievableIt says what has to be true. About three hundred providers are registered today against more than fifteen hundred pending, and nobody knows where that settles. Twenty calls off the published register is the cheapest way to find out, and it comes before the build rather than after it.
It does not say implementation will be productisedIt assumes it. Days to first value appears nowhere in this workbook because it cannot be modelled, only observed. The delivery capacity section on Team Roadmap is the closest thing to a test, and it is a constraint rather than a proof.
It does not say churn will be six per centSix per cent implies gross revenue retention of about ninety four per cent, against a ninety one per cent benchmark for bootstrapped software companies. This plan models better than median retention, on no evidence, because it has no customers yet. Set the scenario to Downside and churn doubles.
It does not say the competitive gap holdsCable sits inside a platform with Canadian distribution and a Schedule I bank behind it. The gap is Canadian regulatory content, submission and price, and it is a finding with a shelf life measured in quarters rather than years.

4ORM FINANCE · MASTER PRO FORMA
Trust account evidence infrastructure for Canadian regulated business. Six year model, all figures Canadian dollars.
Built 5 August 2026. Start here, then read the Summary and the Dashboard. Nothing in this workbook assumes you have seen anything else.
WHAT THIS BUSINESS IS
The product4orm runs the daily proof a regulated business owes when it holds somebody else's money. It reconciles the ledger every morning, keeps every version of every artefact with who approved it and when, assembles the regulatory filing from the record rather than from folders, and produces the examination bundle on one button.
The duty it sells againstSince 8 September 2025 every payment company registered with the Bank of Canada that holds end user money must know daily whose money it holds and how much, reconcile that against the safeguarding arrangement, and produce the evidence on request. The duty is daily, it belongs personally to the provider, and it cannot be handed to the bank.
The three modulesSafeguard is the daily ledger and reconciliation. Comply is the rest of the duty set on the same statute. Trust is the same engine on a monthly cycle for property management trust accounts under a provincial regulator.
What it is notNo money movement of any kind. The system reads balances and evidences them. That boundary keeps 4orm outside the registration regime and outside the penalty ceilings that fall on its customers.
HOW THE MODEL IS BUILT
Customers come from the marketEvery customer number is an addressable population multiplied by a penetration rate, on the Market tab and the Control Panel. Nothing is typed as a customer count.
One selector moves the whole planThe scenario on the Control Panel drives penetration, price, churn, pay, development spend, overhead, Assist attach and services revenue together. Downside, Base and Upside.
Nine switches, all on the Control PanelGrants, services, the second regulator, the Assist tier, research credits, churn, capitalisation of the build, the loss carry forward and the venture debt facility. Each one removes everything it claims to, including the cost.
Costs have driversEvery overhead line is a fixed amount plus a rate multiplied by headcount, customers or new hires. Change the hiring plan and insurance, tooling and recruitment all move by themselves.
Revenue is customers times priceA customer live at the end of a year contributes half of that year. Hiring uses the same convention, so a mid year hire costs half a year. There is no growth rate assumption anywhere.
2026 is a part yearFive months, August through December. Pay, overhead and revenue all scale on it.
Two escalators, not onePay and subscription price rise three per cent a year from 2028. Overhead inflates separately at two and a half, because overhead rises whether or not anybody gets a raise.
Contingency is a real cost lineTwenty per cent of the cost base falling to eight, inside operating expenses rather than in a footnote, and each development component carries its own overrun rate on top.
Two rounds, and the first must stand aloneA pre-seed of two million and fifty thousand dollars across five tranches in 2026 and 2027, and a seed of three and a half million in 2028. The test that matters is whether the pre-seed reaches ten reference customers without the seed. THE EIGHT NUMBERS A PLAN LIKE THIS USUALLY FLATTERS, AND WHERE THIS ONE SITS
TaxAlberta charges eleven per cent on the first five hundred thousand dollars of active business income and twenty three above it. Both bands are modelled, and losses made in the early years are carried forward to shelter the first profitable ones. Charging the general rate on everything, or ignoring the carry forward, moves the answer by a lot.
GrantsEight programmes are modelled and none of them is awarded. Mitacs is not among them, because Mitacs pays the academic intern and the company contributes rather than receives. Switch the grants off on the Control Panel and the plan still reaches the seed.
The exchange rateOne point four zero six eight, the Bank of Canada daily average. It is the easiest number in the workbook to check, and it is worth checking.
Gross marginServices revenue carries its own delivery cost, and hosting is charged at a rate taken from a published like for like benchmark rather than chosen. The result is about seventy five per cent, not the eighty five a slide would claim.
Sales and marketingSixteen per cent of revenue against a twenty per cent median for this revenue band. The OpX tab gives the reason: the buyer list is published, so less has to be spent finding buyers.
The research creditThirty per cent of eligible engineering pay plus eighty per cent of contract payments, plus the Alberta innovation employment grant, every rate sourced. This is the one line in the model that runs in our favour.
ChurnSix per cent of customers leave each year, gross revenue retention is reported against benchmark, and setup revenue is floored so that a departure cannot create a refund.
The switchesEach of the nine switches removes everything it claims to, including the cost. Turning off the second regulator removes those customers, their setup fees, their recurring revenue and the build spend together, at source, rather than deleting one revenue row and leaving the rest in place.
This model sets out what has to be true for the plan to work: how much of which market, at what price, against what cost base, funded by how much capital. Every one of those can be tested against what actually happens, and the two that would fail first are market penetration and days to first value.

SECTION 3 · THE BORROWING FACILITY, AND WHY IT IS A SWITCH RATHER THAN A DECISION
What the facility costsFive hundred thousand dollars at eight per cent, drawn in August 2026 and repaid when the seed closes in the fourth quarter of 2028, costs about eighty three thousand dollars in interest across the whole period. The money is returned and the ownership is unchanged.
What the same money costs as equityFive hundred thousand dollars sold at the fifty cent price gives up four point seven two per cent of the company, permanently. On any exit above two million dollars the facility is the cheaper of the two.
Why it ships switched offThe plan funds on the two million and fifty thousand dollars of equity alone, so every figure in this workbook is an equity only figure unless the switch is changed. The facility exists so that a slow quarter is a financing question rather than a hiring question, and it is drawn only if it is needed.

Lines one to four confirm the wiring inside a tab is intact: each total still covers every one of its parts, so an inserted row or a shortened sum range is caught. Lines eight, nine and ten compare two tables that are built independently and do not read each other, which is the harder test. Lines five, six and seven are thresholds the plan sets for itself and can read REVIEW. Line six reads REVIEW today, because the plan reaches the seed decision with less than the twelve months of cash it sets as its own threshold.

1Cash reconciles to the profit and lossPASSThe cash balance at the end of 2031 has to equal every dollar of capital put in, plus the debt drawn and less the debt repaid, plus every dollar of profit or loss, plus the two adjustments for spending that is not cash. A failure means money is appearing or disappearing between the two sections.
2Operating expenses tie to their five partsPASSTeam, build, sales, overhead and contingency have to add up to the operating expenses total. A failure means a cost line has been added or removed without the total following it.
3Team cost on two tabs agreesPASSThe total cost of the people on the Team Roadmap tab has to equal the team subtotal in operating expenses. A failure means a role was priced in one place and not carried into the other.
4Revenue ties to its partsPASSTotal revenue has to equal subscription revenue plus setup and services. A failure means a revenue line is being counted twice or not at all.
5Delivery capacity covers the rampPASSThe number of new customers the plan signs each year has to be within what the implementation team can actually take on. A review reading means the sales plan is ahead of the hiring plan in at least one year.
6Runway at the seed decisionREVIEWThree other tabs call twelve months of cash the warning threshold for starting a raise. This is the only place in the workbook that enforces it. A review reading means the seed conversation would start with less than a year of cash.
7Every year fundsPASSThe cash balance has to be above zero at the end of all six years. A failure means the plan runs out of money.
8Pre-seed tranches tie to the capital raisedPASSThe five equity tranches listed on the Capital Structure tab have to add to the pre-seed capital the Control Panel feeds into the model. These are two separately built tables and neither reads the other, so a difference means one of them was edited on its own.
9Grants tie to their schedulePASSThe grant line in the profit and loss has to equal the total of the eight programmes on the Non-Dilutive Capital tab. A failure means a programme was added or switched off without the profit and loss following it.
10Month by month cash agrees with the yearly cashPASSThe Pre-Seed Deployment tab spreads the same money across twenty months and runs its own running balance. Where it ends at the close of 2027 has to equal the closing cash the yearly profit and loss reports for 2027. The two tables are built separately and neither reads the other, so a difference means one of them has been edited on its own.
One careless edit can break a link without producing an error message. These ten lines catch that. Open this tab after any change and every line except line six should still read PASS. Line six is a threshold the plan sets for itself rather than a tie between two tables, and it is explained in the legend below.
