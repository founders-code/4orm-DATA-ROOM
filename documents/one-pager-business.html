Where it hurts today

An examination pulls two people off their jobs for a week to rebuild a file from an inbox, a drive and someone's memory. A complaint becomes a reconstruction exercise because nothing recorded why this product suited this customer at the time. Supervision is an annual scramble, and the reasoning walks out the door when the person who held it leaves.

What changes

The reviewer's file is produced on request, from records written as the work happened. The reason a product was chosen is captured while it is fresh, dated against what was known then. Incomplete files surface the day they occur, across everyone acting in your name, and the record outlasts whoever made it and transfers with the file. This is a substitution against money you already spend on file review, compliance consulting and reconstruction hours, not a new budget line.

What it never does

4orm never holds or moves client money, so there is no treasury review and no change of bank. It never decides, and never ranks products for the professional. It never performs your independent review. The duty stays with the firm; what goes away is the part that is fragile and impossible to reconstruct under pressure.
