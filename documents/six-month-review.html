The Six-Month Review

How an advisor engagement is measured, reviewed, and either carried forward into a defined role or concluded, at the six-month mark.

01
PURPOSE

Why this review exists

Every advisor joins 4orm on an initial six-month engagement. That engagement is not open-ended. It is a defined period with defined deliverables, at the end of which both sides decide, honestly, whether to carry the relationship forward into a defined role.

This document sets out how that works, so there are no surprises on either side. It is read together with the advisor's Engagement Letter and Compensation and Term Schedule, which govern the binding terms.

THE KEEPER'S QUESTION

This review is the formal version of the question the Mission Anchor asks of everyone. Six months in, we answer it together, on the evidence: would we fight to keep you, and would you fight to stay?

ABLES

The deliverables, and how they are tracked

The Engagement Letter for each advisor names specific deliverables, tied to the lane that advisor leads (for example capital markets, brand and marketing, or technical and talent search). Those named deliverables are the basis of the review.

Each deliverable is tracked as the work happens, not reconstructed at the end, against what was agreed, its target date, its current status, and its outcome:

03
THE
REVIEW

The review, at six months

Who is in the room.

The advisor, the CEO, and the Advisory Council, or a designated subset of it. [to build out: name the reviewing members]

When it happens.

In the final month of the initial six-month term, with enough runway to make and communicate a decision before the term ends.

What is assessed.

Delivery against the named deliverables; contribution to the mission and to the advisor's lane; fit with the culture and standards set out in the Mission Anchor; and the value of continuing versus concluding.

How it is decided.

An honest, blameless review. The CEO holds the final decision, informed by the Advisory Council. [to build out: any voting or threshold, and how the decision is recorded]

04
OUTCOMES

The outcomes

The review reaches one of three outcomes, communicated to the advisor before the term ends.

Carry forward into a defined role.

The engagement converts to a defined, ongoing role, with scope, compensation, and any equity or token participation set out in a new or amended agreement.

Extend for a defined period.

Where the fit is right but the deliverables need more time, the engagement is extended for a further defined period with refreshed deliverables.

Conclude with respect.

Where it is not the right fit, the engagement concludes at the end of the term, with respect and generosity both ways, consistent with the Mission Anchor.
