1.0
MISSION

This statement records my personal commitment to 4orm Finance, the company I am incorporating in Alberta and intend to lead as Founder and Chief Executive Officer. It is intended to make my commitments explicit and durable to my co-founders, to the Advisory Council, to incoming pre-seed investors, and to the team that will build the Company alongside me.

The mission I am committing to

4orm Finance exists to help the Canadian financial system become independent and sovereign in its own self, to protect Canadians from inflationary asset erosion and currency debasement, and to build the regulated infrastructure that allows Canadians to hold, transact in, and settle against tokenized bank deposits and, in due course, other tokenized real-world assets.

I commit to that mission as the organizing principle of my role, and as the standard against which my decisions, my time, and my economic interest in the Company should be measured. The Mission Anchor in the Articles of Incorporation binds the Company; this statement binds me personally to the same standard.

2.0
EXCLUSIVITY

Full-time, exclusive engagement

4orm Finance is and will be my sole operating commitment. I have wound down or stepped back from every other operating role I previously held in order to dedicate my full professional capacity to this Company. For the duration of my service as Chief Executive Officer, I will not take on other operating roles or competing commitments.

3.0
TIME & CAPACITY

Time and capacity

This is my full-time work, in every meaningful sense of the term. The day-to-day, the weekly cadence, and the long-horizon work, all of it. I commit to availability and presence sufficient to discharge the responsibilities of the Chief Executive Officer of a regulated Canadian enterprise.

4.0
CAPITAL & RISK

Capital and risk borne

I have funded the Company's pre-incorporation work personally and brought in its first outside capital. I commit to continuing to bear personal financial risk on the Company's behalf where appropriate and within reason, including by absorbing dilution and by continuing to align my compensation to Company outcomes rather than to short-cycle personal returns.

5.0
FIDUCIARY

Fiduciary standards

I commit to the standards of care, loyalty, candour, and good faith owed by a director and officer to the Company and its shareholders under applicable law. On incorporation of the Company under the Business Corporations Act (Alberta), I commit to the specific fiduciary duties of directors and officers under that statute.

6.0
ACCOUNT- ABILITY

Accountability on the same terms as my co-founders

I subject my own equity to the same reverse-vesting framework that governs the rest of the founding team, four-year quarterly vest with a one-year cliff, quarterly KPIs, with the secured carve-out and time-served credit documented in the capital structure workbook. My 69.70% (13,940,000 Class A common shares) is not exempt from the same accountability standard I ask of others.

7.0
CONTROL

Control on the same class as my co-founders

In Version 14 of the capital structure, I hold my 69.70% as Class A common with a 1:1 voting mechanic, the same class and vote as every other shareholder. I do not hold a differential voting class. The Control Preservation Framework (Founder Consent Rights, Board Composition Rights, CEO Tenure Protection, the Voting Alignment Covenant (Founders' Voting Agreement v14), Supermajority Thresholds, and contractual Mission Anchor incorporation) protects the mission of the Company through contract and governance, not through a differential voting class.

I commit to exercising the authority granted by the Control Preservation Framework in service of the Mission Anchor, and not for personal control unmoored from the mission or the Company's long-term integrity.

8.0
THE BARS

Mission Anchor adherence

I commit, personally and explicitly, to every commitment of the Mission Anchor as set out in the Articles of Incorporation, including each of the following Absolute Bars:

8.1 No participation in account closures, freezes, restrictions, or service denials directed against any Canadian for lawful political expression, faith, belief, association, or affiliation, and no action on extra-judicial freeze instructions absent a final court order with judicial review available.

8.2 No sharing of customer data, content, metadata, or transaction information with any government, regulator, agency, or non-judicial body absent a final court order with judicial review available.

8.3 No participation in industry-wide debanking coalitions, shared blacklists, or informal coordination among financial institutions to deny service outside of legal process.

8.4 No participation in programmable-restriction infrastructure that enables third-party conditional control over a Canadian's own assets, accounts, or transactions.

8.5 No surveillance of customer activity for non-legal purposes, and no collection, retention, or use of customer data beyond what is operationally necessary or strictly required by law.

8.6 No sale, monetization, licensing, or sharing of customer data for advertising, profiling, behavioural conditioning, social credit, or ideological assessment of any kind.

8.7 No participation in social-credit, behavioural-conditioning, or ideological gatekeeping systems sponsored by government, industry, or private actors.

8.8 No deplatforming, denial of service, or adverse action against any Canadian for protected expression, sincerely held belief, faith, or lawful affiliation.

8.9 No commingling, rehypothecation, pledging, or use of customer assets as collateral for Company operations or third-party financing.

8.10 No quarterly earnings targets, quarterly growth projections, or any short-cycle financial metric used as a primary directional input for strategy, hiring, product, or capital decisions.

8.11 No retaliation against any person for the principled exercise of refusal under the Mission Anchor.

8.12 No surrender, waiver, suspension, or weakening of any provision of the Mission Anchor under political, commercial, regulatory, or shareholder pressure outside of law.

These are not aspirations. They are the operating standard of the Company, and they apply to me as plainly as they apply to any officer, director, employee, or agent of the Company. I will refuse to direct, vote for, or authorize any action inconsistent with any of them, and I will exercise my refusal right under the Founders' Voting Agreement and my Founder Consent Rights to that end.

9.0
HORIZON

Horizon

My commitment is multi-year and runs through the full arc of the Company's growth, from incorporation through pre-seed, seed, Series A, and beyond. I am not building this for the next milestone or the next round.

10.0
WHAT I WON'T DO

What I commit not to do

  • I will not take on other operating roles or competing commitments during my service as Chief Executive Officer.
  • I will not compromise the Mission Anchor for capital, partnerships, or expediency.
  • I will not act in ways that prioritize personal economic interest over the Company's long-term integrity.
  • I will not avoid the difficult conversations, with co-founders, investors, regulators, partners, or the team, that the role requires.
  • I will not ask of any co-founder, employee, or advisor a standard of commitment I am not first prepared to hold myself.
