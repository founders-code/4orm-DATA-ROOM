Profit, Cash and How Long the Money Lasts

Two questions, answered from the same arithmetic: does the business make money, and does each funding round reach the next. The company turns profitable in 2028 and earns $58,393,173 before tax in 2031. Cash is positive at every year end, reaching $86,616,099 by the end of 2031 before any payout to investors.

Profit from 2028

The first two years lose a little, then the company turns profitable in 2028 and grows from there. The whole cumulative funding gap the two rounds have to cover is only $472,480.

Why tax arrives early

The early losses are small, so they only shelter $472,480 of profit, which is used up inside 2028. That is why tax appears in the first profitable year rather than several years later.

Cash lags profit

Revenue is invoiced and collected forty five days later, so the amount owed by customers grows every year and ties up $12,979,349 of cash across the six years. A year that ends above zero can still contain a month with nothing in the account, and this plan has eight.

Support is modelled but not counted on

Eight grant programmes worth $775,000 and two tax credits sit inside the plan, and none of the grants is awarded. Switched off, the plan still reaches the round, on a deeper low point of $1,123,710.
