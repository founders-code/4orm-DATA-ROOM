How the Product Works, in Plain Terms

4orm Finance does the daily check a business must do when it holds money that belongs to other people, and it keeps unchangeable proof that the money was all there. Since September 2025 a Canadian payment firm has owed that proof every day, and at almost every firm the work is still done by hand in a spreadsheet. The product does the check, keeps the record, and hands it to a government examiner in the exact form asked for, even when the examiner is asking about a single day that happened well over a year ago.

It never touches the money

The product is not a bank and holds no customer funds. It never signs anything for the customer and never decides anything for them. What it sells is the proof the law asks to see.

A record that cannot be edited

Once a day's proof is written down it cannot be changed and cannot be deleted. If something needs fixing, the correction sits beside the original, which stays readable, so the full story of any fix is part of the record.

A test anyone can run

Export one month of records, wait a year, export the same month again, and compare. If the two copies are not identical, the record was not truly locked. That single test is built into the product rather than promised on paper.

One build, more than one rulebook

The same five screens can carry a payment firm reporting daily to the federal regulator or a property firm reporting monthly to a provincial body. Adding a new set of rules is meant to be a written setting, not a new piece of software.
