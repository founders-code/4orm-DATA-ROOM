HOW IT WORKS

How it works

Your name on the list.

The moment you help, you are on it. Paid or unpaid, three days or three years, it does not matter. One hundred names, and no more.

Ten tokens each.

One thousand legacy tokens in total, ten to each of the first hundred. That is the entire pool, allocated to the people who showed up first.

A share of the profit, every year.

The legacy class is intended to receive 2% of 4orm Finance net profit annually, indefinitely, divided across the tokens and paid out yearly. Ten tokens is one percent of the pool, and at the scale 4orm is built for, it is designed to become a genuine inheritance over time, not a one-time bonus.

Built to outlast you

Held in trust.

We are working to place the legacy tokens in a trust, for tax efficiency and to protect the people who hold them.

Passed to your family.

We are designing custody so tokens can be held securely and transferred to loved ones and family over time. A legacy in the real sense, not a slogan.

The list is real.

It is created and tracked today. As the structure is approved, the list comes to fruition, name by name.

THE 2% EXPLAINED

The 2%, and what it could mean.

A closer look at the legacy token, grounded in our model and cast forward.

Why the legacy token exists

The legacy token is a way of doing business differently. Some people come in early and, though they do not spend a lot of time with us, they truly move the business. This is how we honor the people who believed in us at the beginning. We believe relationships should last, so we tie those relationships into what 4orm becomes, for good, and pass that on to the people they love.

What the 2% is measured on

The legacy class is intended to share 2% of the Company's profit, paid yearly. "Profit" here means what is left after the business pays to build and run itself, our EBITDA: revenue minus development and operating costs, before financing and tax. We do not expect a profit in the early build years. In our model the Company is investing and running at a loss while it stands up the platform, and profit begins to build as the institutions come online. The honest way to read the 2% is to start the clock at the point the Company is profitable, and grow from there.
