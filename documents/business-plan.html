The Business Plan

4orm sells one engine four ways: a free check for everyday people, a paid seat for professionals, accounts for organizations, and a safeguarding record for payment firms that must meet a new federal duty. Canadian regulators have moved the test from whether a form was filled in to whether the file can show why a decision suited the customer, and today most files cannot. The plan starts in Ontario mortgage brokering and reaches $71.1M of Canadian revenue by 2031 on the base case, first making money in 2029.

What the company sells

The record of what happened in a big consumer financial decision: what the person wanted, what was known, what options existed, what was recommended and why, and what followed. It is assembled from systems a firm already runs, and the consumer gets a readable copy of the half that concerns them. The company sells to the regulated business; the free consumer check earns nothing and exists to build the relationship the paid product is sold into. The first paid market is Ontario mortgage brokering, then automotive finance to test that the same engine carries across.

The market, counted not guessed

The plan counts populations rather than estimating a market. Three layers owe the same kind of duty: 745 payment firms must protect the money they hold now, 25,682 organizations under provincial regulators owe it too, from law and real estate to mortgage, travel, insurance, funeral homes and insolvency, and 38,000 businesses are supervised for money laundering. No revenue figure is a population multiplied by a price. By 2031 the base case reaches 3.0M free everyday users, 7.2 per cent of Canada, 40,000 paid professionals, 400 organizations and 110 of the 745 payment firms, which is 14.8 per cent.

The money

Revenue runs $0 in 2026, then $0.9M, $6.1M, $18.7M, $40.7M and $71.1M through 2031, Canada only. The business first makes money in 2029 and keeps $36.9M after running costs in 2031, a margin near 81 per cent. The team grows from 3 people to 24 by 2029 and 60 by 2031, against 75 without the workflow savings. The pre-seed is $2,050,000, with $1,500,000 open now and closing in May 2027, followed by a $3,550,000 round in September 2027 priced at $30 million. Cash turns positive after the 2027 round and stands near $65M by the end of 2031.

The one bet, and the honest limits

The plan carries three cases: Base, a slower Low case where distribution does not reach profit inside the plan, and a Blue-sky case where a few national networks make it take off. The product, price and law are the same in all three. The whole plan rests on one claim: 40,000 paid professionals by 2031, 35,500 of them arriving through 110 networks rather than one at a time. If the networks do not activate their members, the plan is the Low case. No customer has yet paid the price card, and proving that is what the next stage is for.
