The product

What We Are Building

This is a plain description of the software. A Canadian business holding money for its customers has owed a daily proof since 8 September 2025, and at almost every firm the work is still done by hand in a spreadsheet. The software performs that daily check, keeps the proof so a stored record cannot be changed, and produces it later under the rule that applied on the day.

The duty in five steps

Money that belongs to a customer comes in, sits apart from the firm's own money, and has to be proved daily against what the bank holds. The proof then has to be kept unchanged and shown later, and steps four and five are where firms go wrong.

What is on the subscription

One subscription carries six features, including a sealed record store, a model that keeps two dates on every entry, the daily comparison of the two lists, and a library of duties held as dated content. A second rule maker becomes content to load rather than a code change.

Three claims a reader can test

Each claim can be checked against any rival: export a month, export it again a year later, and the bytes should match, or the claim is false. Another test asks the system what was held on a past day and checks that the answer carries the old rule, not today's.

Where it runs and what it never does

Everything is stored and processed in Canada on infrastructure the customer controls, with an outside security review each year. The company never holds or moves customer money, signs no filing for a customer, and decides nothing for them.
