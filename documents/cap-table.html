Who owns what today, what the raise is, what each tranche costs in ownership, and where every figure came from.

WHAT THIS WORKBOOK IS
The question it answersWho owns 4orm Finance today, what happens to that as the money comes in, and what each way of raising the next dollar costs. It is the ownership side. The money side is the master pro forma.
The raise, in one line$2,050,000 of pre-seed equity across five tranches, of which $550,000 is already committed, then a seed of $3,500,000 in Q4 2028 priced at $30,000,000 post-money. A $500,000 borrowing facility sits behind it and is switched off in every published figure.
What is not in itRevenue, cost, cash and runway. Those live in the master pro forma, which this workbook reads from rather than repeats.

LEGEND · EVERY TERM AND EVERY COLOUR ON THIS TAB
Subscription agreementShares bought at a set price today. The money is equity from the moment it lands and the number of shares is known immediately.
SAFESimple agreement for future equity. The money can be spent now and the shares are issued later, when a priced round sets the price.
Valuation ceiling, or capThe highest company value at which a SAFE is allowed to convert into shares.
DiscountThe reduction on the next round's share price a SAFE holder receives instead, if the ceiling does not bind. Fifteen per cent here.
Most favoured nationIf a later pre-seed investor is given better terms, the earlier holders receive the same terms automatically.
Venture debtBorrowed money at a stated interest rate. It is repaid rather than converted, so it costs no ownership at all.
Bridge loanA short loan that carries the company until a larger sum arrives, then is repaid out of it.
Reverse vestingShares are issued in full today, but the company can buy them back at a nominal price if the holder leaves early. It is how founder shares are protected.
Option poolShares set aside to hire with. It is created out of the pre-money, so the existing holders carry it rather than the new investor.
Post-moneyWhat the company is worth immediately after the new money goes in.
Fully dilutedCounting every share that exists or is committed, including the option pool.
OpenThe investor for that tranche has not been identified yet.

SECTION 4 · WHAT THIS ENGAGEMENT IS NOT
No blockchain, no token, no wallet, no custody, no settlement and no money movement of any kind. The system reads balances and evidences them.
