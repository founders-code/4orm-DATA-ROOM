Operating Thesis

OPERATING THESIS · a small senior core and network distribution
Six commercial motions, each with a named Canadian comparable, a revenue logic and a failure mode. 4orm Finance does not scale headcount linearly with customers. Every motion below states what would make it fail, because a thesis without a failure mode is a slogan.
4orm does not scale headcount linearly with customers. It concentrates capability in a small number of senior people and uses networks, strategic partners, workflow automation, self-service and outside specialists to create leverage. The financial model must prove that thesis rather than assume it.
