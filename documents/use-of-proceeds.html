Where the Money Goes

The money raised pays to build the software and to serve the first customers by hand before the work is turned into a repeatable product. Total spending across 2026 and 2027 is $4,657,404. The largest single bucket is the cost of serving those first customers. This spending buys the company to its first paying customers, to a working product, and to the point where it earns more than it spends from 2028.

The largest bucket is hand delivery

The cost of serving the first customers is the biggest line at $1,386,462, almost thirty per cent of the two year spend. That is because the earliest customers are delivered by hand, one at a time, before the work of setting them up is turned into a repeatable product.

Building the software and paying the team

Team pay and benefits take $1,100,693, or about a quarter, after research credits. Outside engineering and build take $825,000. Together, people and build are where most of the rest of the money goes.

The other lines

A reserve for the unexpected is a real line at $505,711, not a rounding. Sales and marketing take $423,788 and day to day running costs take $415,750. Nothing here is padded, and the reserve is kept on the face of the plan rather than hidden.

What the spending buys

The money carries the company to its first paying customers in early 2027, to design partners running the software in production, and on toward ten installed reference customers. The plan turns profitable in 2028, so beyond the low point the money buys speed and cushion rather than survival.
