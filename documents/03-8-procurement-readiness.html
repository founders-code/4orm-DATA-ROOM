CONTROL POSTURE

What a bank's vendor-risk team will ask for

Selling regulated infrastructure to an institution means passing its vendor-risk, security, and procurement review. This page sets out the control areas those teams test, the target 4orm is building to, and the honest status of each today. Nothing here is claimed as certified; the targets are commitments, and the statuses are current.

AREAINTENDED CONTROL TARGETSTATUS
Security and audit (SOC 2)SOC 2 Type II program over security, availability, and confidentiality; independent audit once the platform is in operationPlanned
Data residencyCustomer and transaction data hosted in Canada; documented data-flow and sub-processor mapTarget set
Incident responseDocumented incident-response and breach-notification plan; defined severity tiers and timelines; regulator and customer notification pathsPlanned
Custodian of recordA licensed third-party custodian holds assets under segregation and custody-tiering; named per engagementProvider-agnostic
Deposit issuer of recordThe cash-leg deposit liability is issued and held by a chartered bank; 4orm holds no deposits and no CDIC-insured fundsBy design
Access and identityRole-based access, least privilege, audit logging, and SSO for institutional usersPlanned
Business continuityBackup, recovery, and continuity plan with tested recovery objectivesPlanned
Exit and portabilityDocumented data-export and off-boarding path so an institution can leave with its records intact; no lock-in of the canonical ledger dataCommitted
How to read this

This is a pre-build readiness statement, not a certification. 4orm is not yet operating, so SOC 2 and similar attestations are targets to be completed once the platform is live. The custodian-of-record and deposit-issuer-of-record rows are stated provider-agnostic on purpose; specific partners are named per engagement.

Forward-looking; control targets are commitments to build, not current certifications, and are subject to change and to receipt of required regulatory approvals. Not an offering document.
