The growth model

To one million people, and the firms behind them.

4ormIQ is free for the consumer, so it can grow the way a consumer app grows. 4orm earns its revenue from the regulated firms that pay for the record. The target is one million consumer users by 2031, and the paying firms that ride alongside them.

The benchmark

KOHO, Canada’s most-used neobank, went from roughly 500K users in 2022 to 1M in late 2023 and 2.5M by 2026, on about $250M of annual revenue. A free, trust-first check has the same consumer dynamics. 4orm targets its first million in five years rather than nine.
