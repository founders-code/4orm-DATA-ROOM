The behaviour underneath the market. Time & cost evidence

What the record is worth.

The price rests on saved work. At C$30K a year, a firm saving 500 hours at C$60 an hour already clears the cost. Comparable tools across banking, lending and compliance show the scale of what a complete, retrievable record removes: days collapse into hours, and hours into minutes.
