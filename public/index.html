How it settles

Three things settle as one.

01 · TITLE

The record of ownership

4orm operates the ledger of record and the token registry, so ownership changes hands cleanly and is recognized.

02 · CASH LEG

A tokenized bank deposit

Issued and held by a regulated Canadian bank as its own liability. 4orm connects to it; 4orm does not issue it.

03 · RULES

Identity, eligibility, sanctions

Checked before the transfer and embedded in the settlement, not bolted on after the fact.

When all three commit together, delivery versus payment, the transfer is final the moment it settles.
