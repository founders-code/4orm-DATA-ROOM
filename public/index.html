01

Who we are

4orm is building counselling-centred intelligence tools that help financial businesses guide clients more clearly, understand them better and preserve a clearer record of why decisions were made.

02

What we do

4orm plugs into a business’s existing apps and compliance systems. The business gives its clients a value-add tool to privately explore their goals, circumstances and risk profile, model outcomes and build a clearer understanding of short and long-term risks. The client chooses what to share, giving the professional greater context for a better-informed recommendation. This creates an auditable record for both the business and the client across the suitability and decision-making process.

03

The problem we are solving

Major financial decisions are hard for people to fully understand, while businesses face rising compliance costs, suitability expectations and pressure to explain why recommendations were appropriate. Too often, firms guide the client once and then spend time rebuilding the record afterward.

04

Why it matters

4orm helps the consumer understand what is happening while giving the business the evidence to prove why it happened. That means better client understanding, lower discovery time and clearer records as regulatory expectations increase.
