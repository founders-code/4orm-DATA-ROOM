The Challenge

The missing layer is client understanding.

Businesses are investing heavily in technology to better understand and protect their clients, reduce compliance costs and respond to growing regulatory pressure. But too often, the answer is more data, more forms and more documentation.

1

More documentation proves what was collected, not what was understood.

That can help prove what information was collected. It does not necessarily prove that the client understood the decision, the risks, the trade-offs or why a recommendation made sense for them.

The gap
