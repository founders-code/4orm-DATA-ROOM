The Build Partner's Track Record

This is Speer Technologies' own portfolio, the record of work the build partner has already shipped. It matters because a young company usually carries the open question of whether its team can build and ship a real product. This team has already built for a national airline, a large bank, major retailers and a regulated Canadian crypto trading platform, which answers that question before the money is spent.

A long list of shipped work

The portfolio runs across finance, aviation, health, retail, property and connected vehicles. Named clients include TD Bank, Air Canada, IKEA, GreenShield, Netcoins and a property firm called Grata whose platform now serves more than sixty thousand residents.

Two clients that matter most to 4orm

Grata sits in property management, one of the buyer groups on 4orm's own list of future customers. Netcoins is a regulated Canadian crypto trading platform, which is exactly the kind of firm 4orm plans to sell to, so the team has already worked inside a real customer's world.

Relationships that run for years

Several engagements ran two, four or five years, with Grata and OnPath at five years each. Long relationships suggest clients stay once the work is delivered, rather than trying the team once and leaving.

How to read this document

This is Speer's own marketing portfolio, not an endorsement of 4orm Finance, and 4orm has not independently checked the individual claims. It is included so a reader can judge the track record of the firm doing the building.
