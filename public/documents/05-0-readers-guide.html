Everything comes from one workbook

Every figure in the folder is calculated in a single master financial model. If a number is not in that model it does not appear here, and nothing is estimated to fill a gap.

Read the one-table document first

The plan fits on one page: money in, customers, revenue, margin, earnings, cash and staff across six years. Two documents, the month-by-month road and the self-check paper, are written for a doubter rather than a browser.

Losses sit in brackets, in plain sight

A cash low point of $778,960 is shown as ($778,960) rather than buried. Each share of the market is measured against a named group of firms, never a blended average that flatters everyone.

One version of the plan is shown

Nine on/off switches can turn parts of the plan off, but every figure here is the full plan with everything switched on. The self-check document shows what happens when a switch is thrown.
