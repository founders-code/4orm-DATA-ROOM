Market size

How Big the Market Is

The market is counted firm by firm from public registers, priced on one published price list, and every share is measured against a named group of firms rather than a blended average. The group that matters is the 745 Canadian firms that hold customer money today, because holding the money is what triggers the law. By 2031 the plan reaches 214 of them, which is 28.7 per cent.

Why the base group is 745, not 1,562

1,562 payment firms are registered, but the law attaches to holding customer money, and 745 of them do. The other 459 are waiting for approval and hold no customer money yet, so they are shown separately and carry no revenue.

Four ways to size the same market

Each layer is smaller than the one above: a fully-priced ceiling of $1.77B, the repeating market headline of $840,471,950, a near-term qualified figure of $491M, and the 745 fund holders priced at $72M. The top layer is never used as the base of a share.

The target sits inside a believable range

Comparable firms reached between 0.4 per cent and 31 per cent of their own named populations. At 28.7 per cent by 2031, the plan sits above the early curve and below the best outcome observed.

An earlier, larger claim was withdrawn

An earlier claim of 224 firms inside three years is dropped: it would take six signatures a month for twenty five months from a standing start, which is not deliverable at any funded staffing level.
