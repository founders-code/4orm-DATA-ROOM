Why 4orm?

Where we come from, the gap we saw in the Canadian market, the moment Canada is in, and the network we are building to bridge it. This is the why behind everything else in this room.

01

Where we come from

We come from a background in the education space in finance. Over the last six years, working with thousands of retail investors, we saw the demand clearly: people wanted to participate in digital assets, in tokenization, and in the movement of value. The appetite was there. What was missing was the rail beneath it.

The Canadian financial market did not have the technology, the regulation, the innovation, or the collaboration to provide a mission that could create the network and infrastructure for the interoperability and settlement finality of digital assets and tokenized value, across every institution in Canada and businesses alike. So we set out to build it. 4orm Finance is developed by KCS Capital, an independent technology and research firm, and operated by 4orm as a separately governed, regulated entity.

02

The gap we saw

There is plenty of innovation happening in Canada. The problem is that almost all of it lives in small, independent ecosystems. Those pockets of capital, value, and assets are illiquid. There are new and creative ways for assets and finance to interact, but the marketplace that would give them reach, access, buyer and seller capital, and institutional liquidity, is simply not available.

Canada already has the pieces. Credible asset issuers in real estate, private credit, energy, mining, and precious metals. Stablecoin and tokenized-deposit infrastructure. Qualified custodians. What Canada does not yet have is the bridge: institutional liquidity, a bank-aligned secondary market, an investor pool, and a settlement layer that connects those issuers to the buy-side. That bridge is 4orm.

The heart of it

At 4orm, we do not believe in self-made anything. There are enough passionate companies already moving in the right direction. We just want to connect them with the institutions of Canada.

03

Canada's moment

The question of whether tokenized settlement works in Canada has been answered. On March 6, 2026, the Bank of Canada completed Project Samara, Canada's first tokenized bond trial: a C$100 million tokenized bond issued by Export Development Canada with RBC and TD, settled in Bank of Canada wholesale central-bank money, across the full lifecycle from issuance to redemption. CIRO published its Digital Asset Custody Framework in February 2026. The CSA elevated tokenization to a national initiative with Project Tokenization. Alberta created Canada's first regulatory sandbox under the Alberta Financial Innovation Act.

The market structure question has moved from "if" to "who builds it." The institutional infrastructure layer that connects Canada's banks, custodians, and issuers does not yet exist. 4orm is built to be that layer. No regulator has reviewed, endorsed, or approved 4orm; Project Samara is directional industry proof, not a 4orm result.

04

What we are building, and how we are different

Canada does not need another crypto exchange. It needs a settlement layer. Most tokenization companies are building tokenized assets. We are building the institutional infrastructure that allows Canada to operate tokenized finance at scale, safely: digital settlement, tokenized CAD deposits, real-world-asset issuance and registry, and a regulated marketplace, designed end to end for Canadian regulatory frameworks.

We are not here to just mosey along. We are here to disrupt the way business is done in our space, to bring tools to the institutions that carry Canada's regulatory burden, and to do it with high-touch client service, accountability, and transparency. A rising tide raises all ships, and our job is to lift the whole ecosystem, not to wall off a corner of it.

05

How we lead

At 4orm, we are going to lead the way in collaboration. We are going to be confident in what we know and humble in what we do not. We are going to do everything with passion, integrity, and grit. We know this is going to be a long and hard road. We know there will be many challenges, many naysayers, and many threats. We are building for that, not around it.

06

The culture and the team

With the culture and the team we have at 4orm, we have a diverse, professional group, masters of their craft in each department, a board of advisors, some of the best legal minds in Canada, some of the best advisors in Canada, and some of the hungriest individuals anywhere to solve some of the most complicated and critical problems in the Canadian fintech space.

At 4orm, you will notice something right away. Nobody is scared to be bold. We are very selective with who we work with, and brutally honest with each other, holding a high standard. We realize that people make mistakes. We realize that everybody brings their problems into a company, but we are here to serve, edify, collaborate, and change the future.

At 4orm, we are not just a company. We are a family. We are the formers of the future, forming the technology that can provide stable finance, sovereign capital investment, and infrastructure for Canada.

Who this is for

4orm is not for the chosen few. It is for the few that choose to be part of something that could forever change the future of Canadian fintech.

07

The bigger picture

Canada is the fourth-largest natural-resource country in the world. Imagine what would be possible if Canada could tokenize even a fraction of its natural resources and create a secondary market of liquidity. Imagine the tools and the resources that could create, to support growth, to serve the debt, and to open new resources and opportunity for Canadians. Alberta's resource base alone is one of the largest on Earth; a portion of that value could be put to work today, without selling the asset, by converting future revenue into regulated, liquid instruments.

What 4orm is

4orm is not just a marketplace, and not just a technology. 4orm is the marketplace that will connect all real-world asset value to all institutions, and to any company that wishes to bring value to the Canadian regulated marketplace.
