Where the cheque size sits

Four recent Canadian rounds set the scale: two early rounds at $1,000,000 and $1,300,000, and two later rounds at $3,300,000 and $4,000,000. This raise of $2,050,000 is bigger than the two early ones and smaller than the two later ones. That is where a round asked to carry a company from a design pack to ten paying customers sits.

The early price against the band

The middle value for an early Canadian company in 2026 is between $5,000,000 and $6,000,000. Buying in at 25 cents values 4orm Finance at $5,000,000 before the money, which sits inside that band. Buying in at 50 cents values it at $10,600,000 after the money, which sits above it.

The planned 2028 round

The middle value for a company at the later stage in 2026 is $24,000,000. The planned 2028 round at $30,000,000 is about a quarter above that, two years out, and it is priced against real recurring revenue rather than a forecast.

Why five cheques, not one

Alberta is thin at this stage. In the first half of 2025, forty five Canadian early rounds shared $33,200,000, an average of $740,000 each, and Alberta took seven of them against Ontario's eighteen. Splitting this raise into five cheques fits a market that does not write one large early cheque easily.
