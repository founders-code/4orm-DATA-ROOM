The cash road, month by month

Cheques arrive on the last day of August, September and October and each funds the month after, so the closing balance sits below the $25,000 safety floor until November, when it reaches $53,380. That thin stretch is the single thing to watch in this window.

What has to happen

A build partner offsite runs in late August, a second build partner instalment falls in October once its scope is settled, and grant work is funded every month. Each item releases a cheque, removes a cost, or opens the sales register.

Decisions owed in the window

About $22,500 of spending is awaiting a yes or no, covering tax registration, counsel to read the build partner's new proposal, cloud and development costs, and insurance and bookkeeping. A further $73,900 is deferred to early 2027.

What the window is judged on

Success is the account staying open above its floor, the build scope being settled before the October cheque is paid, and the evidence layer being put in place. The evidence layer and its two dates cannot be added later at any price.
