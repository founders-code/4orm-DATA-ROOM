The Founder's Commitment

This is the founder's personal commitment, and it holds to the same one line the company is built on: protect the people whose money is being held. The founder sets the price, sets what the company will and will not build, and keeps the right to say no. The commitments below are the ones the founder is willing to be held to.

What I am pointing the company at

I keep this company pointed at the people whose money is being held, not at the firms that buy the software. The daily proof that the money is all there, and whose it is, is the whole of the job. If a decision does not serve that, it does not get made.

What the company will not do

The company holds no customer money, signs no filing for a customer, and decides nothing on their behalf. The duty stays where the money is. I will not move the company across that line to win business.

How I will treat the numbers

Every figure past today is a management estimate, and I will say so. Prices with nothing behind them stay held rather than raised. The plan is written to be argued with, and I will keep printing its weak points on its face.

Where my own power stops

I set the price, I set what we will and will not build, and I can say no. I cannot write the content of a customer's rules, and I cannot approve a release on my own. Those limits are on me by design.
