Six years on one page

One line a year, from the 2026 build with no revenue by design, to $105,276,946 in 2031. If any of those six years is wrong, the model built on it is wrong too.

How the raise releases

Money comes in five instalments, a $50,000 first cheque and four cheques of $500,000, each released against evidence rather than a date. A $500,000 borrowing facility sits beside them and is switched off in every figure in this folder.

The lowest cash point

The deepest the account goes on the road to the 2028 round is a shortfall of $778,960 in May 2028, and it is stated plainly rather than smoothed. With grants switched off, that low point deepens to $1,123,710, and the plan still reaches the round.

What it does not claim

The plan does not claim the share of firms is guaranteed; it states the share it needs and names the population it is measured against. It also does not claim delivery is fast or that customer losses hold at the assumed rates, and it says so.
