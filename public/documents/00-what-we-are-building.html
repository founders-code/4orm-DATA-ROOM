THE PROBLEM

A transfer cannot become final across institutions

When a tokenized asset moves from one institution to another, it cannot become final and legal today. Even tokenized money has this problem: a stablecoin can move on a chain, but it cannot be moved cleanly from one regulated institution to another and be custodied there with finality. Real-world assets cannot move from place to place at all. The token is not the hard part. The hard part is making the transfer final, legal, and recognized on the other side.

2.0
THE MODEL

Three things travel with any asset

For a transfer to be final across institutions, three things must move together. 4orm moves all three in one settlement.

01 · Title
Record of ownership
Ledger of record and registry.
02 · Cash leg
Tokenized deposit
Issued by the bank as its own liability.
03 · Rules
Identity and eligibility
Sanctions and jurisdiction, enforced on transfer.
Delivery versus payment
Settlement finality

When all three settle together, the transfer is final the moment it commits. 4orm issues the record of title, not the money. The bank issues the deposit as its own liability.

3.0
THE WEDGE

Why bank deposits first

Every settlement has a cash leg; you cannot swap an asset without money on the other side. A tokenized bank deposit is that cash leg, in regulated Canadian dollars institutions already trust. So the deposit is not the small first step before the real thing. It is the on-ramp that pulls everything else through. Own the cash leg and 4orm becomes the settlement asset every other transfer needs: bonds, receivables, real estate, and eventually stablecoins from other issuers.

4.0
THE BUILD

Where this goes

Phase 1
The cash leg. Stand up the tokenized bank deposit as the settling money.
Phase 2
Interoperability proof. Settle a second asset against the cash leg.
Phase 3
Conditional settlement. A verified milestone releases payment instantly.
Phase 4
Open network. Other issuers plug in and settle through 4orm.

The direction is already proven abroad by the UK Regulated Liability Network and Fnality, and at home by the Bank of Canada Project Samara. None is building the neutral, bank-integrated, Canadian-dollar layer that connects them.

THE LINE

What we are, and what we are not

4orm is
  • The record of title for tokenized assets
  • The settlement layer that makes a transfer final across two institutions
  • The connector to a bank-issued cash leg
  • Neutral; no single bank owns it
4orm is not
  • A bank, and does not take deposits
  • A custodian of client assets
  • A stablecoin issuer
  • The AML officer of record
