Outreach plan

How We Reach Customers

This sets out who the company can reach, the filter to apply before a single call, the five ways in, and what a real answer looks like at the end of each. The contact list holds 1,886 named payment firms and 226 named people across 86 credit unions and community banks, and none has been contacted yet. About 702 of the payment firms are the ones that actually owe the daily duty.

Filter before the first call

The register lists many firms, but the law attaches to holding customer money, and only about 702 of the 1,472 named firms do. Reading that flag off the register and splitting the list first avoids the most expensive mistake: calling a firm that does not owe the duty.

Five ways in, ranked by cost

Calling fund holders directly and working the approval queue carry the first two years. The reviewer channel and partner organisations have to carry the rest, and the reviewer channel, which the plan leans on hardest, has never been tested.

A qualified answer is a fact, not a feeling

Every conversation is meant to end in a name, a date, a document requested or a number. The twenty discovery calls settle one question: what share of the counted firms will agree to pay, and how long a new customer waits for its first result.

What the list cannot do yet

It carries no flag for which firms owe the duty, no contact name for any payment firm, and no published roster of the independent reviewers the plan depends on. Each gap is a reason to record what is learned as the calls happen, not a reason to wait.
