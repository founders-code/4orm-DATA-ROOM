The cost base

Where Every Dollar of Cost Goes

Running costs are built from five parts and the cost of serving customers from eight components, and almost nothing is set as a flat percentage of revenue. Each part is calculated from the staff, the customers or the build that drives it. In 2031 running costs are $23.3M, the cost of serving customers is $23.6M, and the gross margin reaches 77.6 per cent.

Built from the drivers, not from revenue

Team cost follows the hiring plan, the build follows a set monthly price, and overhead is a fixed amount plus a driver. Only sales, marketing and the contingency reserve move with revenue, and both are named as such.

Why the margin rises

Nothing gets cheaper. The margin climbs from 64.3 per cent in 2027 to 77.6 per cent in 2031 because low-margin services shrink as a share of revenue and the record-rebuild line, the most costly to deliver, is designed to expire.

A small team on purpose

The internal team grows to 47 people, only eleven of them in engineering, with contracted delivery carrying the volume under internal review. Nothing is capitalised, so the earnings line is not flattered by moving cost off it.

Selling costs about eleven per cent, every year

Sales and marketing runs from 10.9 per cent of revenue in 2027 to 11.0 per cent in 2031, flat enough that the plan is not buying its growth. Separation of duties keeps rule-writing, code and customer sign-off in different hands.
