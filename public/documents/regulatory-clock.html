The Dates That Drive the Business

This is a dated list of every law and rule the business depends on, each read at its official source. The date that matters is not in the future: it is 8 September 2025, when the duty to record each customer's position at the close of every day came into force. A firm that has not kept that record is not approaching a deadline, it is already behind one.

The date that governs the sale

The rule the sale rests on came into force on 8 September 2025 and asks for a ledger showing each customer's funds at the end of every day. That duty has fallen due 344 times already, and none of those days can be recorded after the fact.

What each date requires

An annual report is filed with the Bank of Canada each 31 March, money laundering penalties rose on 26 March 2026, and the Bank of Canada began naming firms in violation on 12 June 2026. A separate model risk guideline takes effect on 1 May 2027 and does not apply to payment providers, though it is often misread as if it does.

Why a late start cannot be repaired

Rebuilding a daily position from bank statements after the fact is a separate, priced project, and a reviewer can tell a reconstruction from a record made on the day. Every day that passes turns a subscription into a larger reconstruction job at a higher cost.

What was withdrawn

An earlier version stated that a first mandatory independent review falls on 8 September 2028 and reads three years back. The regulation was read directly, requires a review at least once every three years, names no first date and imposes no lookback, so the claim was withdrawn.
