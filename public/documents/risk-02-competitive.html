The one name to watch

AutoRek already computes a daily position on money held for customers and already writes Canada on its coverage with nothing Canadian behind it. What it lacks is the Canadian rule content and a North American sales presence, which is localisation on an existing engine rather than new building.

The cheapest threat needs no software

A Canadian adviser could put a fixed price on the yearly report pack tomorrow, because that is a pricing decision, not a build. The answer is to make the adviser a paid channel before one of them becomes a competitor.

What actually settles it

Two things retire the whole board: three signed Canadian enterprise customers and one completed outside review that used the evidence pack. Until those exist, every competitive claim is a position held by argument.

A window, carried as a window

No vendor holds a Canadian safeguarding customer today, and the strongest rivals are pointed at the United States and Europe. That is an opening, not a moat, and its length is nobody's to set.
