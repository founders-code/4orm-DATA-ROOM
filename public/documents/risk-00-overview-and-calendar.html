Six areas, thirty seven scenarios

The risks split into rules and policy, competition, whether customers adopt in time, building the software, funding, and reliance on key people. Each scenario is scored on how likely it is and on how much it would hurt.

Six worst cases, three facts

The six top risks reduce to three honest facts: nothing has been sold yet, the urgency is real but the deadline is not, and the hardest parts of the product to copy are cheap to build now and costly to add later.

One calendar underneath everything

A single dated list runs from the 2026 close to the 2028 round: the funding cheques, the first trial customers, and the months where cash runs short. When a date moves, every risk that keys off it is checked again.

Everything traces to one model

Every figure comes from the company's financial model dated 16 August 2026, which checks itself in sixty five places. Two of those checks read review by design, both in the funding area.
