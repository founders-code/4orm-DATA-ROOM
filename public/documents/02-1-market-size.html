Market size

How Big the Market Is

This paper measures the market three ways and shows the maths between them, so an investor can see which number is a real claim and which is just a boundary. All the Canadian firms that hold money for other people are worth about $840.5M a year at list prices. Of those, about $490.6M sit with firms that already have money set aside to spend today. The plan aims to bill about $105.3M of that in 2031, which is one dollar in eight of the whole market.

What you are looking to know

  • Which of the three market figures is a real claim about this company and which is just an outer limit
  • How the biggest number is cut down, step by step, to the smallest
  • How many of the firms are actually counted from a public list versus estimated
  • What single assumption the plan leans on hardest, and what would settle it
  • What share of the market the plan is truly claiming, by money and by number of firms
  • How the price gets to $105.3M when the plan wins only a fifth of the paying firms

Three numbers, not three markets

The whole market, the part with a budget today, and the part the plan actually wins are one market filtered twice, not three markets to add together. The paper draws each one inside the one above it so the shape narrows the way the argument does.

Every cut is shown

The paper walks from a top figure of about $1,770.7M down to the $105.3M the plan bills, showing each deduction: pricing each firm to its size, removing firms counted twice, removing firms with no budget, and the four in five who buy from somebody else.

The assumption to argue with first

The weakest number is the guess that 35 in every 100 of the smallest firms have money set aside today. It governs more of the gap than the other four bands combined, and twenty conversations with small firms would turn that guess into evidence.

A counted list, not an estimate

About 60 per cent of the firms are counted from a published government register, and the 745 payment firms the plan sells to first sit entirely inside that counted part.
