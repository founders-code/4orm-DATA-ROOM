What it is

A free check that explains what you are looking at: the options, the trade-offs and the information that is missing, before you sign. Afterward you hold a readable record of what was discussed, agreed and why. The regulated business pays for it. No consumer is charged, and no consumer information is sold.

Where it helps

Today the explanation happens out loud and is never written down, so you are asked to decide before you understand. Nobody can tell you later why this product and not another, because the reasoning lived in one person's memory. 4orm gives you the reasons in plain words while it is fresh, and a copy you keep. If circumstances change, the change is dated against what was known before, so the two are never confused.

What it will not do

A missing record is not proof that anyone did anything wrong, and 4orm never says it is. It gives no financial advice: the professional keeps the recommendation, and the firm keeps the duty. What it saves you is the hours of re-explaining your situation, and the months a dispute takes when nobody can produce the file.
