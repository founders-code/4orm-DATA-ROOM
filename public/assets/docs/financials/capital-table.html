REVERSE VESTING MECHANICS — how repurchase rights work
Concept (Founders/Advisors)All shares issued at signing (legal ownership). Company holds repurchase right over unvested portion. As shares vest each month, they become non-repurchaseable.
Advisor repurchaseAdvisors paid $0.001/share at issuance → company repurchases unvested at $0.001/share (returns their money). Repurchase right exercised within 90 days of departure.
Founder repurchaseFounders received shares at $0.0001/share (nominal) → company repurchases unvested at $0.0001/share. Repurchase right exercised within 90 days of departure.
Investors — NO reverse vestingPurchased shares and SAFE rights follow the signed agreements and applicable law. No investor reverse vesting is assumed by this model.
Louise exceptionLouise Royer FULLY VESTED at incorporation — no reverse vesting, no transfer restrictions.
Termination triggers (advisors/founders)Reverse vesting activates on: (a) voluntary resignation, (b) termination for cause (breach, misconduct), (c) failure to perform advisory duties, (d) death/disability (Board discretion may waive).
Change of controlSingle-trigger acceleration: 25% of unvested shares accelerate on CoC. Double-trigger: additional 50% if terminated within 12 months of CoC. Founders may negotiate 100% acceleration.
Board discretionBoard can waive repurchase right at their discretion (amicable departure, good leaver treatment). Requires majority Board approval.
INVESTOR TRANSFER RESTRICTIONS & RIGHTS — how investor shares are controlled without reverse vesting
Transfer restriction schedule4-year with 1-year cliff. Month 0-12: NO transfers permitted. Month 12: 25% becomes transferable. Month 12-48: linear monthly release. Month 48: 100% transferable.
Applies toDon’s purchased shares, first-tranche shares, SAFE rights and seed shares: restrictions, if any, must be established by signed documents.
Right of First Refusal (ROFR)Company (or Board designee) has first right to purchase any shares an investor wishes to transfer. Investor must offer company matching terms first. 30-day company response window.
Co-sale / tag-along rightsIf a founder sells shares to a third party, investors have the right to sell their proportional share in the same transaction at the same price.
Drag-along rightsIf holders of >66.7% of shares approve a company sale, all other holders (including investors) are dragged along at the same terms. Prevents holdout blocking a good exit.
Approved transfereesStandard permitted transferees (spouse, family trust, estate on death, wholly-owned entity) may receive shares without triggering restrictions, provided they sign joinder to same restrictions.
Change of controlAll transfer restrictions terminate on acquisition, IPO, or company dissolution. Shares become freely transferable at that point.
EnforcementAny transfer in violation of these restrictions is VOID and does not transfer legal title. Company may seek injunctive relief.
